# S-62: Rank Expand: the Upwork Playbook (30 modules, 23 videos, the 90-day path)

## What it teaches
- Upwork is the on-ramp: a free freelancer profile, then daily written pitches to posted client jobs, each with a Loom video under three minutes. Volume is the lever the course leans on (3, then 5, 7 and 10 applications a day).
- Profile conversion is the only craft taught: a results headline, the first two sentences (the part shown in search), three portfolio pieces, an underpriced starting hourly rate, and free Google and HubSpot certificates as trust cues.
- Early reviews are the real product. The course says to take cheap, quick, easy-to-please clients first, then raise rates and move to flat fees once the reviews exist.
- The cold-email side is a separate asset: a sending domain warmed for three to four weeks in Instantly, plus Stripe for invoicing. No conversion, reply or win rates appear anywhere in the text.
- Transfers to agency outbound: the problem, agitate, solve, proof, call-to-action copy and the "give them two options" close, which the outbound-email skill already covers. Nothing else carries over.
