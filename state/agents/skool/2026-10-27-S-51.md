# S-51: Small sales rooms: Sellique (classroom, 2 videos, posts), cold-email-community and Enrich feeds

## What it teaches
- Bookings depend on three things: a reason to answer now, proof with specific numbers ("we got X in Y using Z"), and a guarantee that makes the offer risk-free but not free (Sellique owner, 17 Jul).
- A professional sender address and a two-line opener beat long discovery questions. The founder's first Leadbeam week went from zero calls over 45 cold calls to two calls after making both changes (intro transcript).
- Diagnose by volume: a week at about 150 dials a day with no bookings points to the market, one or two bookings a week points to the pitch (intro transcript).
- Lead lists are built, not bought: scrape companies from Google Maps or search, generate name variants, find people by keyword, then fuzzy-match the results by hand (cold-email-community owner, 30 Sep 2025).
- Setter pay is a benchmark for teams: $10 per showed appointment, $75 per closed deal, $2,500 to $4,000 a month OTE (Sellique owner, 8 Aug).
