# S-23: Matt Ryder: Sales Management SOPs and the Business Mastery finance primer
## What it teaches
- Waterfall lead distribution: fill the top rep's lead quota first, then the next, and swap leads between reps based on results and booking confirmations.
- Plan backwards from the revenue number: revenue to closes to shown calls to bookings to conversations to pickup rate to lead volume, with 3 to 5 KPIs per role and consequences for misses.
- Split show rate into no-show (ghost), reschedule and cancellation, and stop reschedule links inside about 4 hours of the call. Then the "don't touch it yet" rule: isolate the variable before changing anything.
- Management cadence: weekly WIP (projects only, no prep ends the meeting), monthly department reports, a CEO rollup, a decision log and an After Action Review after every launch.
- Finance primer is standard: COGS, OpEx, gross vs operating vs net profit, Assets = Liabilities + Equity, "run on cash, report on accrual". The CloudCRM worked example is arithmetically correct. Also copy-paste failed-payment chasing texts (insufficient funds, bank block, wire, PayPal, cancel message).

## Skill or workflow candidate
Triton's clients (firms with outbound sales teams, M1 lane): a "sales funnel diagnostic" workflow. It takes a client's weekly numbers (leads, dials, connects, bookings, shows, reschedules, no-shows, closes), splits no-show/reschedule/cancel, runs the reverse-math plan and flags the one variable that moved before recommending any change. Needs only a CSV or sheet export from the client's CRM and a short Claude skill with a calculator script. Also usable as a prospect demo with dummy data. For Proteus itself: none.
