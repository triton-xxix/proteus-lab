## What it teaches
- Launch thin, then build from what members ask for: a Start Here course of three lessons (welcome post, navigation, a level poll), each linked to a live community post so the first engagement happens on day one.
- About page: who it is for, outcome, what is inside, one call to action (join). Group description gives 150 words but only the first 19 to 20 show in Discovery. Cover 1084x576, icon 128x128.
- Start free (quoted 20% conversion) and move to paid later (quoted 2 to 4%), unless you already have an audience.
- Guest talk: YouTube is the traffic source. Make "one problem, one solution" search videos, put the call to action in the first 30% (average view is about 3 minutes of 10), and give a lead magnet that lives in the Start Here classroom, so the About page has one action: join.
- Q&A call: keywords must now sit in the About text because Skool removed the separate keyword field. Lead magnets can be the free classroom itself. Mention what premium members get, in the free area.
