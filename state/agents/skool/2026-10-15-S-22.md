# S-22: Matt Ryder: objection handling text (money-first matrix, logistical to fear, the SOPs under each Loom)

## What it teaches
- It is a sequence with a gate, not a pile of word-tracks. Sort the objection into logistical (money, partner, "think about it") or fear. Clear every logistical one first, then enter fear. Fear is never touched while a logistical item is open.
- Money sits at the root: "think about it" and "talk to my partner" are treated as money in disguise. The pivot phrase is "Money aside, if this could be the answer for you...", then confirm budget, then payment plan, then "is that door closed?". Partner gets "if they were on board, would this be the solution?".
- Pitch the highest price first, keep payment plans out of the pitch, and only bring in instalments after the prospect says it could be the solution.
- Fear is handled in "cycles" of 5 to 10 minutes. Each loop ends on the same question ("what do you need to do to put yourself in the best position to achieve your outcome?") and turns three dials up a notch: emotional charge, time pressure, assertiveness. Late cycles tie it to identity and consequences, then "push away" ("you don't have to decide today") once they say yes.
- The text under each Loom is an AI-generated SOP summary (objective, key steps, cautions). The real matrix is a file (id 2f622517...) and the live calls (about 25 hours of sessions including a 480 minute Session 4, plus audio-only live calls) are not in this text. The "30 hours" is mostly video we have not read.
