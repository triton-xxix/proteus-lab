# S-39: Title and opening triage of the 272 YouTube lessons (vault rooms and 15 Oct pulls)

## What it teaches
Most lessons are no-code tutorials. A trigger (form, email, webhook or inbound call) starts a chain of nodes in n8n or Make.com, a model node turns messy text into fields, and the chain writes to a sheet, CRM or calendar. Voice agents run the same loop: speech to text, a model, text to speech, and a tool call that books a slot. The commercial hook is speed to lead: answer within a minute or lose the lead. The digest covers 239 transcripts, not the 272 the group names, so 33 lessons are not in this digest.

## One thing worth trying
A stdlib-only speed-to-lead stub in sandbox/: a local HTTP server takes a form POST, stamps it, puts it on a queue, and a worker logs the gap to a stub first contact. Send 200 local POSTs, then report the p95 latency and how many clear 60 seconds. About 80 lines of Python, under 30 minutes, with no accounts, keys or spend. It measures our own plumbing only. It says nothing about whether faster contact converts, and the output should say so.
