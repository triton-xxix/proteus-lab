Read note: the file is 9,970 lines. I read lines 1-1300 in full (23 Mar to 22 Apr), then targeted the rest by searching for pricing, discovery, Growth Boost, conversion, tiers and tools, and read those posts in full. Most of the feed is daily filler (tips, check-ins, welcomes, Friday leaderboards). Nothing structural looked missed, but I did not read every post.

## What it teaches
- Small paid-community owner (a 400+ member crochet Skool, plus a free-ish "how to run Skool" community) teaching a calm, consistency-first method.
- Three-post daily rhythm: quick morning post (easy to answer), an engagement post (question, poll, leaderboard, member spotlight), one main teaching post. Batch drafts in a draft course because Skool cannot schedule.
- Start Here course doubles as a research and engagement device: each step asks a question, answers go into a member spreadsheet (source, goal, struggle, stage, DM sent).
- Positioning: a one-sentence community statement built from members' real struggles; About page must say who it is for, their problem, what they get. Price "the change", not hours or access. Offer early (tier, VIP, 1to1), even with few members.
- Discovery: engagement, activity, owner presence and low churn lift category rank; keyword search matters more than rank (rank #665 overall, page one for "Skool"). Skool removed the separate keyword box (30 Sep); the About page text now carries the keywords. Growth Boost (Skool-funded ads, 30% commission) now affects search, suggestions and ads.

## Tools they use
- Skool itself (classroom, leaderboard, polls, pinned posts, Discovery, Growth Boost, Efficiency Score).
- Panda, a Chrome extension for Skool (member tracking, scheduled posts, auto DMs, churn view): https://www.skool.com/303-6342/about (her post is a referral link; $12 launch price, $24 after).
- Axiom AI and Postiz named as paid scheduling options (no URLs).
- A member spreadsheet and a monthly audit checklist (in her classroom, not public).
- Brian O'Neill's Mindset Skool video on Growth Boost: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4M6UlnAP9ZA

- Member tracking spreadsheet: a CSV plus a script is the same thing; Panda's Skool-side auto DM has no equivalent here.
- Transcript and reading pipeline (yt-dlp, whisper, Skool pulls) already covers the Growth Boost video.
- Post-writing prompts: Claude Code with a skill. The frameworks skill already has "launch a Skool" material from Triton.
- Anything for Skool-side analytics or Discovery rank tracking: nothing.

## What we would need to fetch
Nothing. Panda is the only tool of note and is paid with a Chrome extension needing a logged-in Skool, so it is out of scope for unattended runs.

## One thing worth trying
Test the "who it is for, problem, what they get" About-page formula as a checklist. In sandbox/, write a small script-free rubric file (about 20 lines) and score three real Skool About pages already saved in field-notes/skool/ against it, noting which have a named audience, a named problem and searchable words. Under 30 minutes, no keys, no accounts, tiny. It only tests whether the rubric separates good from vague pages; it does not test whether the formula grows membership.

## Skill or workflow candidate
Triton's clients and prospects, and Luke ourselves: an "About page and community statement audit" skill. It takes a Skool About page text and scores it for audience, problem, outcome and natural search terms, then rewrites it. Needs only the page text, no keys or accounts. Probably belongs as a section of the existing launch-a-Skool framework rather than a new skill. Not relevant to Proteus itself. The Skool-based stats are too small and self-reported to build a measurable workflow on.
