# S-60: Rank Expand: Local SEO Innovation, How To Dominate AI Search, Off-Page SEO, Measure What Matters, SEO for Beginners, Basic and Advanced courses

Read: all 5 course pages, both index pages, 9 Local SEO transcripts, 5 backlink transcripts, 1 Reddit transcript. Measure What Matters has headings only (no body text, no transcripts pulled), and the Advanced index lists courses whose bodies were not in this group (semantic branching, microsite blueprint, technical SEO, knowledge wiki).

## What it teaches
- Local SEO for AI Overviews is a seven-point checklist: NAP consistency, a full Google Business Profile, review volume and velocity with 48-hour replies, LocalBusiness and Service schema, service-by-town pages with proof content, citations and sponsorship backlinks, then GSC plus incognito checks every 90 days. Verdict against the frameworks: same with examples, nothing the Local SEO framework lacks except the sponsorship-for-a-link tactic and the "ask reviewers to name the service and town" line.
- "How To Dominate AI Search" is a re-skin of a paper (called University of Toronto, 10 Sept) into three plays: hybrid keyword plus semantic research, RAG-style grounded drafting, an accuracy/SEO-fit/conversion checklist. The course admits the paper gives no ranking tactics. New: nothing testable. Mostly slogan.
- Off-page: buy guest posts (DR 25 to 30, 1,000 to 5,000 traffic, about $30 to $150) from a rolodex built from competitors' referring domains in Ahrefs, vary anchor text, point links at the money page so equity flows down the "tree". Cold-email, pay by PayPal. New to us (we have nothing on link buying). The "link juice flows down the branches" claim has no test offered. Contradicts Google's guidelines by his own admission.
- Reddit mentions: find ranking threads with `site:reddit.com "keyword" "worth it"`, post a natural comment, then buy upvotes via Socialplug and have a VA do it. Brand mentions are pitched as a click-through manipulation. New, and the paid-upvote half is manipulation that could burn a client brand.
- Measuring: lesson bodies are empty here. Names pixels from top, share of voice, share of rich results owned, SEO Gets and AccuRanker. Beginner course is a curated link list to LearningSEO.io plus a 30-day plan: repeats standard practice. The presenter says outright there is "no good way" to track AI Overviews for local; the AI Overview claim is therefore untested by the course itself.

## One thing worth trying
Build a stdlib Python "AI-readiness audit" in /Users/triton/PROTEUS/sandbox/ that takes a list of local-business URLs, fetches each homepage and contact page, extracts JSON-LD, and scores: LocalBusiness or a subtype present, Service schema present, areaServed, sameAs, aggregateRating; plus NAP strings in footer versus schema. It tests the one mechanism in the course that is mechanical and checkable (the checklist) and doubles as a prospect demo. It does not test whether any of it causes inclusion in AI Overviews.
