# S-58: Magnetic Memberships: the videos in Steve and Jamie's posts

## What it teaches
- Pick the model by what you already have: free community feeding a high-ticket call book (Model A, needs sales skill and a small engaged audience), or a low-ticket paid community sold straight from the About page (Model B, needs existing traffic, 100 members at $100 is 10k a month). Free-to-low-ticket is said to cannibalise itself.
- First 25 paid members are led, not organic: sharpen who/what/price, launch about 80% built, offer a founding-member bonus, then phone or message a hot list of 10 to 100 people and pre-sell (they report 6 deposits before a launch, 7 sign-ups on day one).
- About page is a sales page, four layers (under 150 character short description with keyword in the first five words; 1,000 character copy: hook, problem, 5 to 7 outcome bullets, proof, price anchor, guarantee; 4 to 7 proof graphics; 2.5 to 5 minute face-to-camera video). Their audit of the 45 all-time Games leaders ranks 12 copy patterns, with specific numbers, authority markers, risk reversal and a named framework as must-haves.
- Discovery and Growth Boost: 11 keyword tags, the same words in name, short description and About copy; cover photo is now the Meta ad creative (headline of 3 to 5 words, one proof line, founder face); Skool takes 30% of ad-converted revenue. Retention comes from owner presence, 7-day challenges, pinning, member-to-member introductions, live calls at a fixed slot, and 15 minutes a day of useful comments in other communities.
- About a third of the 35 files are off-goal (music, meditation, podcast and "entrepreneur tax" talks, a vendor AI-employee pitch, a long founding-member workshop); the method sits in about 14 of them.

## Tools they use
- Skool (Discovery, About page, tiers, Games, Analytics, native live): no URL named, school.com/magneticai is their own offer page.
- OpusClip for clipping long video into shorts and scheduling, said to replace a $1,500 a month team at about $115 a year: no URL named.
- Poppy AI (content repurposing from your own sources) and Gamma (prompt-built landing page or carousel in 60 to 120 seconds, free plan with 400 credits): no URLs named.
- Claude (projects, Claude Code, Cowork scheduled tasks) for "AI employees"; Fathom call transcripts as the input; Notion and Slack for outputs.
- GoHighLevel (CRM) and Zapier alongside Skool; Loom for welcome and reply videos; Canva for QR flyers and cover photos; Asana for the content board; Stripe via Skool.
