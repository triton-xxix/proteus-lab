# S-30: Matt Ryder: the objection course sessions (sampled 4 of 23), the Book of Reframes and the objection matrix
## What it teaches
- A triage order for sales objections. Classify first: fear, logistical (money movement), money, partner, "think about it", or a smokescreen (no real need). Settle the logistical part before the fear part.
- Partner: get "if your partner were here and all for it?", then test whether they'd put a card down anyway (the "Open Wallet" test), else book a follow-up with the partner present.
- Money: "if you had the funding, would this work?", get them to name their own funding routes, then suggest credit, then a payment plan or deposit.
- Fear: once logistics are clear, shift perspective with a reframe or analogy (island and mountain, plane ticket, "what happens if you are in the same spot in 12 months"). Use a fictional third party, not "you", so they do not get defensive.
- Matt's own advice in the live Q&A: do not over-study the theory, nail money and partner, and do role play with a partner a few times a week.

Triton's clients (firms with outbound sales teams) and prospects as a demo. A role-play drill skill: Claude plays a prospect holding a hidden objection type, the rep replies, and Claude grades on the matrix order (did they classify first, handle logistics before fear, avoid pushing a reframe at a smokescreen). The matrix is a real method for the triage order; the reframe scripts are word tracks and are written for high-ticket coaching and info products, so they would need rewriting for B2B outbound and trades.
