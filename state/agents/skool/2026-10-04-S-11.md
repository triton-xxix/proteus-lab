# S-11: Voice agents 1: Brendan compares every AI phone caller platform, and which to choose

## What it teaches
- Every AI phone caller is a cascade of separate parts: Twilio for the number and the call, a speech-to-text step, an LLM (GPT-4o is his default) for the reply, and a text-to-speech voice (ElevenLabs, Cartesia, Rime, PlayHT). The platforms are mostly glue around those parts.
- So the platforms sound alike because they share the same voice providers. Bland and Air AI say the voice is their own, but he thinks it is probably a third party underneath (his guess, not checked).
- Latency is the gap between the caller stopping and the agent speaking. Better-sounding voice and bigger model both push it up. He measured it by phone, 5 to 10 calls per platform.
- Platform differences are really about packaging: native SMS, calendar and CRM hooks, visual flow builders, white labelling (Synflow only), text-chat testing (Retell). Vapi is the developer end (any model, any voice); Synflow is the agency end.
- Cost is per minute plus an optional monthly fee, and the voice and the LLM are the two big cost lines.

## Tools they use
- Vapi (vapi.ai): developer-first voice agent platform, custom LLM and voice uploads.
- Retell AI (retellai.com): fastest in both tests, native SMS, text-chat testing.
- Synflow (Synthflow): agency platform with white labelling and workflows.
- Bland AI: own voices, "conversational pathways" flow builder.
- ElevenLabs: speech provider and its own agents platform.
- Voice Flow, Vogent, Air AI, Voice OS, Play AI: the other platforms compared. No URLs named.
- Cartesia, Rime, PlayHT: speech providers.
- Twilio: phone numbers and calls under all of them.
- OpenAI GPT-4o (plus Gemini, Claude, Grok as options): the LLM step.
- Cal.com, Go High Level, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zapier, make.com, n8n: integrations.
- Reliable: his own voice agent simulation and evaluation software (sales plug, no URL in the transcript).

## What Proteus already has that does the same job
Nothing for a live phone call: no telephony, no streaming voice loop. Pieces exist: local whisper (the speech-to-text step), Ollama (a local LLM step), and Claude Code with sub-agents for the reply logic. The toolshelf already holds Retell AI (T-0041), Vapi (T-0042) and n8n (T-0043), from the receptionist demo idea H-0085, all still on the shelf with no probe.
