# S-34: Matt Ryder's own recorded sessions (one each: pitch, sales data, roleplay, objections, sales maxing) and the onboarding roadmap
## What it teaches
- Live group coaching for remote closers and setters, mostly high-ticket coaching offers. Members run a roleplay or ask a question and Matt corrects the delivery.
- Objections: handle "money aside, is this the answer?" first, then the partner objection by asking what the partner would be most apprehensive about, then "what if they say no?". A "think about it" is treated as a hidden money objection.
- Discovery: write down the three or four likely answers to each question and word the question to get one of them, so every call is predictable. Memorise the script so you can listen.
- Pipeline: follow up every day for 90 days, then twice a week. His claim is that about 30% of a rep's sales should come from follow-ups, and that most reps never work the CRM's no-shows and lost deals.
- Goals: set a target, reverse engineer it into calls, leads and follow-up buckets. Marketing: split-test one big red button, video against no video, and a plain full-name form.

## Claims to check
- "opt-in to sale is 15%" (with 90 days of daily follow-up): only checkable against a client's own CRM, so ask the first M1 client for their opt-in to sale rate before and after a follow-up cadence.
- "We close about 44% of all the people that we pitch": his own B2B figure for Dialer, unchecked and likely inflated by selection (other figure in 065 is 60% of qualified, and he says high close rates are partly follow-up counted in). Not checkable by Proteus.
- "15% increase on conversion for landing page. Big red button.": checkable with a split test on any Triton page that has traffic. Without traffic the sample is too small to tell.

## Skill or workflow candidate
Two candidates, both for Triton's clients (firms with outbound sales teams, trades such as plumbing and HVAC):
1. CRM reactivation audit. Takes a CRM export (no-shows, no-sales, lost deals older than 14 days), ranks the people worth a call by criteria, and drafts a follow-up per person. Needs the client's CSV and nothing else. This is Matt's own "reps do not farm the CRM" point (065), and it is a sellable demo for a prospect using dummy data.
2. Call-to-script. Takes 20 to 25 transcripts of a rep's best calls and outputs a script with expected answers per question. Needs transcripts (local whisper handles recordings). Treat the claim that it works as unproven.
Neither is for Proteus or Luke himself. Both overlap with what S-21 and S-23 may already suggest, so build once.
