- The nine offers (Web Builds and AI Workflows sections say "Coming soon"):

## Worth paying for the locked parts?
The free part reads as a funnel: the offer descriptions are generic (an audit, a plan, a list), and the real mechanism for the highest-value offers (the Blueprint, the ranking method, Microsite Empire at 30 days, Agency Engine at 60) is behind the lock, with annual billing the only way to open it on day one. Nothing in the free pull proves the ranking method works, and the "$6,000,000+ revenue" and Stripe-screenshot claims are unsupported here. Not worth paying on this evidence; revisit only if the Microsite Playbook and Blueprint (pull-able on the trial) show a method that works in UK local niches.
