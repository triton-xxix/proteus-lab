# S-42: Skool Homeroom: Rose Colbourne's 451 feed posts (about 56k words, read in chunks)

Read note: I read about 60 percent by line count, all of it that carries method. The rest is about 150 repeat welcome posts (the same "double tap" template), weekly Honor Roll lists, Socialize Saturday and Tattle Tale Friday prompts, and personal stories (a house move, a hospital stay). None of those carry method beyond the mechanism noted below. Dates run 4 Mar to 19 Oct 2026. Rose's own numbers are sparse: 3 founding members on day 1, 30 members at day 16, 100 at day 46 (19 Apr), 222 on 10 Aug.

## What it teaches
- Launch minimum, then let members shape it. Founding offer was $10/month for the first 10 (a 30 Day Growth Sprint), then a Premium and VIP ladder, a $17 add-on (Promotion Report Card) and a $9 Standard tier tried in late Sept. She switched to paid for one week, then went back to free with paid tiers (2 Oct).
- Engagement is taught, not hoped for. Every post is Title, then a specific call to action, then content. Weekly rhythm: Show Your Work Sunday (self-promo), Tattle Tale Friday (promote someone else), Honor Roll (top 5 on the leaderboard get named and linked). Free 1:1 call unlocks at Level 3, which gamifies points.
- Discovery and Growth Boost, relayed from Skool News: up to 11 keywords in Group Settings; cover image, title and bio act as the ad; the About page closes the sale. Growth Boost takes 30 percent on members Skool brings, nothing on your own traffic (2.9 percent card fee only).
- Skool's efficiency score, as relayed (28 Jul): clear niche, price $5 to $200 a month, strong About-page conversion, healthy trial conversion, retention above 80 percent. Free trials are said to convert 40 to 50 percent platform-wide.
- Onboarding as the product: she built a 7-day "First Community Win" sequence live with members, plus an AutoDM that asks new joiners what they are working on.

## Tools they use
- Skool itself: Discovery keywords, AutoDM, calendar, classroom unlocks by level, polls, native video. https://www.skool.com

- Facebook page for Homeroom (Skool advertises on Facebook).

## Skill or workflow candidate
Yes, partly, for Triton's clients and Luke's own Skool: a "post and promo linter" skill. It would take a draft community post or promo and check it against her rules (title first, one specific CTA, hook in the first line, a named moment not an "I help" line, no link where the host group bans them), then return a grade and one fix, as her $17 Promotion Report Card does. Needs only Claude and the rules; no keys. The existing `frameworks` skill (launch a Skool) is the natural home, so add it there rather than building a new skill. Weekly-rhythm scheduling and a rank log are plain spreadsheet work.

## Verdict

A decent small-community owner's playbook (clear packaging, one CTA per post, onboarding as the hook, free to paid ladder) that largely confirms what the Launch a Skool framework already holds, with thin numbers and no tested conversion data; fold the post-linter idea into the framework and move on.
