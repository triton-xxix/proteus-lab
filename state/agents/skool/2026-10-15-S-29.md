# S-29: Email Marketing Community: Klaviyo set-up, pop-up forms, Omnisend (skim)

## What it teaches
- Klaviyo bills on the higher of active profiles or emails sent, and a campaign to N people counts as N sends. A 250-profile list sent two emails is 500 sends.
- Attribution windows decide how much revenue an email is credited with (5 days for email opens and clicks by default). Tightening them to 24 to 48 hours gives a stricter number.
- Pop-up offers can be A/B tested by tagging each sign-up with a discount code. The offer is judged on welcome-series conversion, not on form conversion alone.
- Welcome and abandoned-cart flows are waterfalls of conditional splits on profile tags, with a "placed order zero times" filter so buyers stop getting sales emails.
- Cross-sell and review emails trigger off the fulfilled or delivered order event, so they land after the product arrives.

## One thing worth trying
A sample-size check for the pop-up offer test. Using only the Python standard library, work out how many sign-ups each arm needs to detect a 10% relative lift in welcome-series conversion, at 95% significance and 80% power, with a baseline rate we set ourselves. The room's "after 30 days" read gives no sample size. At a 3% baseline the answer is roughly 50,000 per arm, which means the room's read is not a test. This runs in sandbox/ in under 30 minutes, with no accounts, keys or spend, and is about 40 lines of Python. Not built here, since this digest is the only file this run may write.
