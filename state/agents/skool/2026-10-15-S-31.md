## Claims to check
- "A strong pickup rate (ideally, 15% or more)": no source given. Check against public outbound benchmarks and, for the UK, ask a prospect's own call log; S-21 already flagged a similar figure.
- "Make no more than 20 calls per day from the new number" in week one: a vendor rule of thumb with no carrier citation. Check against Twilio, Telnyx or carrier documentation on number reputation.
- Aloware and Kixie "often struggle to achieve A-Level attestation": attestation depends on the originating carrier knowing the caller owns the number, not on the dialler brand. Check against Twilio's own STIR/SHAKEN and Trust Hub documentation before repeating it to anyone.

## Skill or workflow candidate
Triton's clients (firms with outbound sales teams, and trades such as plumbing and HVAC with call-back or lead-chasing teams): a UK "outbound hygiene audit" checklist skill. It would walk a client through number pool and ramp, pick-up by hour, caller ID presentation, call-length mix, and UK compliance screening, and flag where the US material does not apply. Needs the client's dialler export (CSV) and a UK compliance reference written from Ofcom, ICO and TPS pages with a "not legal advice" line. Needs no keys. Do not build the sales-technique parts (the "send them a custom resource" line is described as pretence to lower the prospect's guard, and the "aligned adversary" frame is persuasion by insult); not something to put in front of clients.

UK differences, from general knowledge rather than these files, so verify before use: there is no STIR/SHAKEN in the UK, so attestation advice does not map; the regime is PECR plus UK GDPR, with TPS for individuals (sole traders and some partnerships count) and CTPS for companies; Ofcom's rules cover caller ID presentation (a valid, callable number) and persistent misuse, including limits on abandoned or silent calls from automated dialers; there is no US-style statewide hours table, so calling-hours advice should come from Ofcom or the DMA code. Do not rely on the PDFs' TCPA content for UK work.
