# S-59: Rank Expand: the Microsite Playbook, AI Directories and the Backlink Rolodex (rank-and-rent and directory sites)

## What it teaches
- Rank-and-rent microsite: one service, one town, one exact-match .com ([service][town].com, $10.44 a year), 50 to 80 static pages (a primary service, a couple of support services, one to two dozen town pages), built by a tool plus a ChatGPT content pass, hosted free on Cloudflare Pages. The money is in obscure long-tail services under the primary term, not the headline keyword.
- Leads are proved with a tracked number (WhatConverts, $30 a month plus about $3 a number) that forwards to a Vapi AI receptionist. Monetised three ways: lease to one local firm, pay per lead, or rev-share. Sites are pushed into Google with Omega Indexer ($60 for 400 credits, subscribe then cancel).
- Directory: a Google Maps pull (DataForSEO or Outscraper) is cleaned, each listing enriched from its own website (attribute true/false/not enough info plus a 50 to 200 word description), then Claude Code builds a static state/city/business site from a fixed template via numbered "gates". Earns by calling every listed business ("is your listing correct?"), claim handling, and selling spots ($97 a year premium, $750 to $1,200 best-of, $29/$99/$499 a month featured).
- Backlink Rolodex: a private app of 800+ guest-post sites with prices; the free part is only the outreach loop (site: check, two-line email, haggle to the sheet price, send a doc, pay after it is live, force-index). The list itself is in a separate login app, tiered by months of membership, and not in these files.
- Contest evidence (61 sites, 15 Sep to 18 Nov 2026): profiles/listings ranked before the site; the market mattered more than the build; a jump is a test, not a ranking. Details are in the locked Empire.

## One thing worth trying
Duplicate-content checker for generated multi-town page sets, in /Users/triton/PROTEUS/sandbox/. It takes a folder of static HTML, splits visible text into sentences, and reports the share of sentences (and 5-word shingles) repeated across pages. Run it on our own docs/ site as a baseline, then on a 12-town page set Claude writes for "emergency plumber [town]" in two modes (naive template swap vs a fact-sheet-first prompt). It tests the one measurable claim in the directory lesson (the checker that rejects repeated sentences across cities) and gives a pass mark for any local-SEO page set Neptune builds for a client. About 25 minutes, pure Python standard library, no accounts, keys or spend. Small.
