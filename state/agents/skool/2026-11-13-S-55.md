# S-55: What Is Skool? videos: title-and-opening triage once the transcribe queue has them (240)

## What it teaches
- Skool ranks communities in its discovery feed on member growth, engagement and retention. The 2024 posts say retention is the metric to chase, and they name it "quality human connection" (the SHR method: seen, heard, remembered).
- The model is a free community first, with a paid classroom or call behind it. The free group is the shop window; a paid course or a Clarity Call is the step up.
- Pricing moved from $99 a month to a $9 hobby tier in July 2025. The 14-day trial is pitched as a window to become profitable.
- Growth Boost (June 2026): Skool runs offsite ads for a community and takes 30% commission on customers it brings in. Customers the owner brings in cost nothing.
- The digest covers 237 of the 240 queued files. Most are live show recordings from 2026 (Eric and Jenna), so the first 150 words are mostly chat; the mechanism sits in the Loom posts and the Growth Boost explainers.

## Tools they use
- Loom: short screen walkthroughs, the format of most 2024 posts.
- YouTube and Vimeo: embedded lesson and show videos. The openings name no tool URL.
- Zoom: the 2025 live calls.
- Canva: images for an About page (March 2026).
- Proof Worth: links outside sources to a public Skool page (February 2026).
- Google Docs or pen and paper: drafting a one-sentence offer (March 2026).

Across all the transcripts, the only URLs named are YouTube, Loom and Vimeo links.

## What Proteus already has that does the same job
Most of it. Local whisper and the transcribe-video skill already produced these 237 transcripts, and the S-39 and S-55 reader pattern already does the title-and-opening digest. The harvester and the keyless YouTube pulls cover the feed side. What Proteus does not have is a way to check a platform's own pricing or commission claims, which is the part worth doing here.

## What we would need to fetch
Free first. The Wayback Machine CDX API (already on the toolshelf as T-0016) is keyless and needs no account. It can pull archived snapshots of Skool's pricing and Growth Boost help pages. Skool's own pages need an interactive browser login, which an unattended run does not have, so they stay out.

## One thing worth trying
A Wayback check of the $99 to $9 pricing claim and the 30% Growth Boost commission. Pull the CDX listing for the Skool pricing page and the Growth Boost terms page for 2024 to 2026, fetch two or three snapshots, and search them for "$99", "$9" and "30%". One Python script of about 40 lines in sandbox, a few dozen plain HTTP calls, no keys, no spend. It runs in under 30 minutes. If the snapshots are thin, the result is "not found in archive", which is itself a finding.

## Claims to check
1. "it used to be $99 a month to create a community" (video of 8 July 2025). Check it against archived pricing snapshots from 2024 and 2025, as above.
2. "you pay nothing for customers you bring on your own" (Growth Boost explainer, 13 June 2026). Check the 30% rate and the own-customer exclusion against the archived help or terms page.
3. "from zero dollars per month to $1,700 per month" (member testimonial, 25 February 2025). Cannot be checked keylessly. Log it as self-reported and do not use it as evidence.

## Skill or workflow candidate
A platform-claim watch: diff archived snapshots of a platform's pricing and commission pages, and flag when a number changes. For Proteus, as a check on platform claims, and as a demo for Triton's clients who depend on one platform's fees. It needs only the keyless CDX API.
