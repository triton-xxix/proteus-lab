# S-10: Scrapers 2: Google Maps contact scraper, Apollo lead scraper, LinkedIn jobs scraper

## What it teaches
All three videos are one pattern: rent a pre-built scraper ("actor") on Apify, call it by API from Make.com, then feed each result's website into a second "contact detail" actor that crawls a few pages for emails, phones and social handles.
Make.com glues it together: run actor synchronously, fetch the run's dataset by ID, iterate, aggregate multiple pages into one row per company, write to Google Sheets (LinkedIn version adds search-then-update to dedupe).
Variants: Google Maps (search term plus location gives business listing with phone and website), Apollo (a search URL plus your own login cookies gives people with verified emails, then AI-written cold emails into lemlist), LinkedIn jobs (keyword and location URL gives hiring companies, which are the leads).
The scraping itself is a paid third-party actor, not the tutorial's own code. The tutorial only sets inputs and wires outputs.
Detection and rate limiting get one hand-wave: proxies hide your IP and spread requests, and scheduled daily batches stay under limits. Nothing on how platforms actually detect scrapers.

## Tools they use
- Apify (actors, store, schedules, proxies, API token): https://apify.com
- Google Maps scraper actor on Apify (by "Curious Coder" per the narrator, named loosely; no URL given)
- Contact Detail Scraper actor on Apify (crawls a site for emails, phones, social links; no URL given)
- Apollo leads scraper actor by Curious Coder, quoted at about $5 per 1,000 results (needs Apollo account cookies or login)
- Advanced LinkedIn Job Scraper actor, rental about $30 a month plus usage
- Make.com (automation glue): https://make.com
- Google Sheets (destination)
- lemlist (cold email sending): https://lemlist.com
- OpenAI GPT-4o via Make module (writes cold email and follow-up)
- Cookie-Editor Chrome extension (exports Apollo session cookies)

## What Proteus already has that does the same job
Orchestration: nothing needed. Claude Code with skills, sub-agents, scheduled tasks and the PreToolUse hook replaces Make.com, and Claude replaces the GPT email module.
Pulls: only keyless YouTube, HN, GitHub and arXiv pulls. No scraper of business listings, people or jobs. The toolshelf has Playwright (T-0015) for headless browsing but nothing for lead data.
Not a fit by charter: Proteus sends one email a week to Luke and nothing else to anyone, so the lemlist cold-email half is out of scope.

## What we would need to fetch
Nothing, and nothing to buy. For understanding the mechanism free of charge: Playwright (already shelved, T-0015) to see what a plain browser gets from public pages, and Apify's open-source Crawlee library (https://github.com/apify/crawlee) which sits under many actors. The paid actors and Apollo cookies are not worth fetching: Apollo cookie use breaches its terms and puts a real account at risk, and LinkedIn scraping likewise.

## One thing worth trying
Contact-detail extraction on a site Proteus owns or a plainly public one: a Python script in sandbox/ that fetches a homepage plus /contact and /about with the standard library, pulls emails (regex plus mailto links), phone numbers (tel links) and social profile links, then dedupes to one row per domain. Run it on 5 to 10 public small-business sites, record hit rate per field and seconds per site. This reproduces the "second actor" that all three videos rely on, with no account, key or spend. Size: about 60 lines, 20 minutes. Respect robots.txt and rate limit to one request a second. Do not touch Apollo or LinkedIn.

## Claims to check
- "$5 per 1,000 result" (Apollo scraper price): check the actor's public pricing page, which Proteus cannot fetch tonight; note the Apollo ToS bar on scraping.
- "not even 2 cents" for two Google Maps listings: check against Apify's published per-result price for that actor.
- Contact-detail scrapers find emails for "most results": measure hit rate per field with the sandbox script above on 10 sites. The videos themselves show an empty email for CNN and for a roofing firm, so the real rate looks far below "most".

## Verdict
PARK The mechanism (paid actor plus a second crawl for contact details plus a dedupe sheet) is simple and ours to replicate, but it is lead generation for cold outreach, which is outside the charter; the one small contact-extraction test is optional, and the detection question the lane asked about is not answered by these videos.
