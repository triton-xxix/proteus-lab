## One thing worth trying
Thin-page check, offline, about 25 minutes, no keys or spend, in /Users/triton/PROTEUS/sandbox/. Their own Builder now warns "when two town pages read like the same page with the city swapped", and the contest showed contact and service pages outranking homepages. Write a small Python script that builds 12 town pages from one template (service, town, population and two local facts per town), then scores each pair with word-shingle Jaccard similarity and reports which pairs a reader or Google would call duplicates. Run it twice: pure template swap, then template plus a per-town paragraph. The number that matters is how much unique text per page drops similarity below, say, 0.5. Small test, gives Neptune a reusable page-uniqueness gate for any local-SEO service build. It says nothing about rankings.
