# S-12: Voice agents 2: making callers quicker, more realistic, prompt hacks
## What it teaches
Voice call latency is a sum of four stages (speech-to-text, the language model, text-to-speech, plus the platform's own delays), and each can be tuned: faster model, shorter max tokens, faster transcriber, cheaper voice, response delays set to zero, interruption threshold left at two words.
Realism is mostly voice choice plus a few switches: background office noise, filler words, back-channel "mm, yeah", and punctuation (dashes, ellipses) that the voice model reads as pauses.
Stability and style sliders trade realism against random glitches and speed (style exaggeration at zero is fastest).
The prompt hacks are deterministic text rewriting inside the prompt: write times, phone numbers, URLs and emails in spoken form, with several examples each, so the voice model reads them slowly and correctly.
Real fixes mostly, but the numbers come from a 2024-era vendor dashboard and the main trade is always speed against quality or cost.
