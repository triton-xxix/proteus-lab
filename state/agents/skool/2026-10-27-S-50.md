## What it teaches
- Offer and about page: a "3 P's" frame of Promise (one tangible outcome), Proof (your own or members' results, tracked before and after), Plan (how they get there). Pain points go in the Proof part. A one-sentence offer comes first.
- Free-to-paid ladder (Dec 2024): a free community with an about page, a "Relief and Belief" classroom, a quick-win classroom unlocked by booking a call, and a roughly $3 low-ticket classroom, all pointing at one win before the paid group.
- Discovery and Growth Boost (June 2026): Skool pays for Meta ads, Meta chooses who sees them and rewards what happens after the click (join, stay, spend). Cover photo, description, about page, onboarding and retention therefore drive reach. Level 2 needs 100 about-page visitors and at least 1 Skool Network signup in 30 days; it unlocks custom ads but does not guarantee spend. Attribution is last-touch per community, so a free-to-paid hop counts as Direct, not Skool Network.
- Traffic comes first: "get traffic" is her repeated point, with data before about-page tweaks. She now advises against 7-day free trials until about $10K MRR. Inactive level 1 members do not hurt discovery ranking, so do not purge them.
- Engagement and retention: Seen, Heard, Remembered; annual plans; perks unlocked over time; $25/week or $100/month "engagement booster" as a service role.
