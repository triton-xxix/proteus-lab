# S-36: 3-hour YouTube Leads: the free steps and FAQ (lead generation from low-view YouTube)

## What it teaches
- Trust beats reach: a buyer needs roughly 7 hours across 11 touches (the "7-11-4 rule"), and 28 weekly 15-minute videos get there far faster than 840 reels.
- Find "nurture pathways": topics and title/thumbnail shapes that repeatedly beat a channel's own average (an "outlier" multiple, read off vidIQ). Repeat them, one wildcard test in five, and end each video on a pitch for the next one.
- One pain point and one outcome per video; title, thumbnail and hook are the same promise; only the hook is scripted, the rest is bullets read off a visual; lead-magnet CTA mid-video, next-video CTA at the end.
- Capture viewers by email, then buy cheap traffic ($1 a day Meta instant forms) to the list and send the list back to the videos. The ad and funnel detail is held back for the $1,000 a month coaching.
- Evidence is thin: a few named clients, one "$50,000 traced to YouTube" figure, no data shown.

## One thing worth trying
Outlier finder, in sandbox/, about 60 lines of Python, 20 to 30 minutes, no account or key. Take 8 to 10 YouTube channels in two Triton lanes (plumbing/HVAC trade marketing and B2B outbound sales training), fetch each channel's Atom feed, and compute views divided by that channel's median for each of the latest 15 uploads. List the videos at 3x or more and group their titles by shape (for example "how to X from day one"). Output is a small table of repeatable topic shapes per lane. Size: small; the feed gives only 15 videos per channel, so it finds recent outliers, not history. The point is to see whether the "nurture pathway" signal even exists in our lanes, where channels are tiny.

## Skill or workflow candidate
Yes, small. For Luke ourselves (Neptune lead gen) and as a prospect demo for firms with outbound sales teams: a "pain point to packaging" workflow that takes a niche, pulls competitor channels, ranks outliers, proposes three nurture-pathway hypotheses, and drafts pain/outcome, title, thumbnail text, a scripted hook and a bullet outline with a mid-video CTA and a next-video CTA. Needs yt-dlp and the keyless feeds only; no keys. The most relevant lane for Triton is his Vlad example: a cold-email team that sends one useful video in outreach and lifts replies, since that fits outbound-sales firms better than a trade firm posting weekly. For plumbing/HVAC clients I would not build it: their buyers search locally, not on long-form YouTube.
