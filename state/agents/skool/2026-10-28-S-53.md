## What it teaches
- Skool gives roughly 30% of new members via its own network; about 70% of that is Discovery search, 10% browse, 20% profiles (8 Apr, 5 May). Search was rebuilt in May with typo tolerance and now reads three things: up to 11 owner keywords, the about page text and the group description.
- Discovery listing needs a cover image, description, about page, one post and three non-owner members (5 May checklist). Default sort became trending (24-hour momentum) on 19 May; "top" rank stays and is harder to game.
- Growth Boost (9 Jun): Skool runs offsite Meta ads for groups, on by default, for a 30% commission on customers it brings; own-audience sign-ups cost only the 2.9% processing fee. Groups get ad spend by an "efficiency score" (23 Jul, 28 Jul): clear niche, price roughly 5 to 200 a month (sweet spot 20 to 100), about page conversion above about 1%, trial conversion 30 to 40%+, retention above 80%. Linked social profiles, direct Skool links in bio and video descriptions, and your own traffic to the about page all raise it.
- Packaging is the lever: cover image like a YouTube thumbnail, group card readable on its own, about page by the NOMAD pattern (Niche, Outcome, Mechanism, Authority proof, Deliverables). Test it on strangers. Free trials are pushed hard (platform average said to be 40 to 50% converting).
- Launch evidence: a 1 min 22 s phone video and no course got 200 members in a week (14 Jul); changing how you mention the community in a video or description is claimed to give 3x to 5x sign-ups from the same views.

## Tools they use
- OBS Studio, free screen and camera recording (26 Apr, from a different creator): https://obsproject.com
