# S-28: Email Marketing Community: agency client-getting, Claude Klaviyo reports, Gmail Promotions
## What it teaches
- Claude reporting: a Cowork skill holds a client registry (clients.json), calls Klaviyo's REST API with a read-only key, and builds a fixed-structure Word report (KPIs, top five, subject-line patterns, 10 recommendations, 8 campaign briefs). The same fixed template for every client is the whole trick.
- Client getting: two videos. Conferences: research 10 to 15 exhibitors, subscribe to their newsletter, redesign their email into a printed PDF audit, hand it over at the booth (claimed 5 meetings from 15 documents). Upwork: cheap early jobs for reviews, then GigRadar auto-bids with a one-line Loom offer (claimed 30% reply).
- Promotions tab: a hidden HTML block in the email, set up by a vendor (Customers.ai Inboxer), said to push Gmail to Updates or Primary. The transcript never says what the code is, only shows a 2 to 3 point open-rate lift on a 2,600-person split.
- The rest (profit-per-pod sheet, Virlo content radar, Kluvos pixel for abandoned carts) is agency admin plus affiliate pitches. Four of the seven videos are sponsored or affiliate, said on camera.

## Skill or workflow candidate
Yes, one: a "prospect audit pack" for Triton's clients and prospects as a demo. For a firm with an outbound sales team (M1) or a plumbing/HVAC business, it would pull their public site, newsletter or follow-up emails, Meta Ad Library presence and Google listing, then produce a customised one-to-two page PDF audit that shows what is weak. This is Gavin's conference method turned into a repeatable, cheap door-opener, aimed at Triton's lanes instead of Shopify email. Needs: public data only, Claude Code, a PDF builder; no keys. Per-message approval rules apply before anything is sent to a prospect. The Klaviyo reporting skill itself: None for us, only worth copying if a client runs Klaviyo.
