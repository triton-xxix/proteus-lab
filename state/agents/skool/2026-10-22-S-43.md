# S-43: Skool Homeroom: Rose's 18 Skool-hosted post videos (about 2 h)

Note: the 21 Mar honor roll video is empty ("You" only). Two others are community chatter with no method (24 Mar moving-house update, 20 Jun search-history post). Real content is in about 12 of the 18.

## What it teaches
- Skool truncates text: group description shows about 112 characters in discovery, a member's about line about 76 on hover. Put the hook first.
- Promote with a specific moment ("11.47 pm, dishes in the sink") rather than an "I help X do Y" line. Take the moment from what you are already feeling and from what members say.
- Mustard rule: promise what members say they want (a plan, accountability), then fold in what they need (mindset work). Check your content against their actual stated problem.
- Keyword work: search Skool discovery for target phrases, log your rank and rivals' in a sheet, track changes over time.
- Tiering, as one owner's example: free, $37/month premium (workshop replays, weekly live, Q&A), $297/year VIP (quarterly session and 1-to-1 audit). Earlier founding price was $10/month.

## Skill or workflow candidate
Luke ourselves and Triton clients launching a paid community: a pre-launch checklist step in the existing "Launch a Skool" framework: character-limit check of group description and about line, a "moment not credential" promo rewrite, and a mustard check (stated want versus real need).
