# S-46: Diary of a Skooler: the 4 h 52 About-page video (build a Skool from 0, journaled daily)

Read in full (535 lines of local whisper output). Whisper garbles some names and product terms ("Fable 5", "11 apps", "Calvin/Colvin Hollywood"), so treat those as unverified. The speaker is a Romanian solo creator with a full-time job, a 3,000-subscriber YouTube channel and a free Skool of about 1,100 members, selling a Romanian-language AI community. Dates run 8 Jul to about 20 Sep 2026.

## What it teaches
- Warm audience first, community second: a free Skool (about 1,100 members, 20% joining from YouTube) was the funnel, and nearly all early paid sign-ups came from it. Launch day: 11 members, $144 MRR, 60 visitors, 60%+ conversion, because every visitor was already warm.
- Founding offer with a hard end date worked: 50% off ($19 against a later $47) for seven days. On the last day, 23 sign-ups from 111 visitors (about 40% conversion), against 6 the day after it ended.
- Per-member Loom welcome video (1 to 4 minutes) plus a "go and introduce yourself" prompt kept the wall from being empty and drove activity; he costed it at roughly 5 hours per 100 members.
- Complexity killed conversion: three tiers, free trials and a $9 plan gave 0 to 1 sign-ups a day. He collapsed to one product (two courses) with monthly and annual, raised monthly to $39 to $49, simplified the About page to images, wins and a one-line promise, and logged 6 sign-ups in a day and 4 to 5% conversion.
- Organic content beat paid ads for him: about $50 of Meta ads bought 2,000 to 3,000 views and roughly one purchase; a beginner explainer video ("AI agents clearly explained") brought the traffic and the right (beginner) members. Growth Boost was not available for Romanian communities, which he confirmed with Skool staff.
