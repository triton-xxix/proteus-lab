## What it teaches
- Sell a training programme people repeat (a 6-week, 15-minutes-a-day challenge as the first course), not courses watched once. His claim is that this cuts churn.
- Tiers: cheap entry ($9) with the first challenge open, later courses locked behind $37 and annual. A "what's next" module walks people up the tiers.
- Engagement: one post per member from level 4 (a training log in his template), members comment their sessions, he likes or nudges. Members value seeing others train.
- About page is the funnel. Six banner slots: plain-words hero image, "no water needed" objection, progression, proof with a low number so beginners feel welcome, real screenshots, contents. Test hero banners as a 72-hour Meta ad set and keep the one that gets the spend, not the one you like.
- Traffic: YouTube Posts tab (2 minutes a post), ManyChat comment-keyword DMs, real-life contacts by phone and text, and Skool's Growth Boost now supplies about 80 percent of page visits.
