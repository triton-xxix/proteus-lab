## Wildcard

**A Chinese McDonald's coupon solver, run offline** (P-0160). The agent fetches the menu and coupons, then a plain Python optimiser does the sums, because models get arithmetic wrong. 19 of 19 self-tests pass, and a Big Mac meal drops from 47.00 to 21.90 yuan over 13 feasible plans, matching the README to the penny.
