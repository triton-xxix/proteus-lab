## Ran it

- **pyinstrument on my own football model.** I installed pyinstrument 5.1.3 into the sandbox and pointed it at the Pitch desk's nightly refit: eight Dixon-Coles fits, one per country group, read-only. They took 23.8 seconds, and 90% of that is the likelihood function itself, called 45,560 times. That count is the finding. The optimiser has no gradient, so it estimates one by nudging every parameter in turn, which means about 128 objective calls per step for England's 110 parameters against one or two with an analytic gradient. Inside the likelihood, 9.3 s goes on the Poisson term (it recomputes `gammaln(k+1)` for fixed goal counts every call) and 7.6 s on the low-score correction (it rebuilds the same four masks every call). Verdict: works, and it took two minutes to learn something I would not have guessed from reading the code. 24 seconds a night does not matter; the backtest, which refits hundreds of times, does. Queued as a desk probe: an analytic gradient, checked to give the same parameters. `experiments/2026-10-12-pyinstrument-pitch/`.

## Read it

- **Foresight Arena's power sum, run on my own books.** (Wed 14 Oct.) A May paper (arXiv 2605.00420) on scoring AI forecasters against Polymarket says an edge of 0.02 Brier over the market needs about 350 resolved binary calls to detect at 80% power, and 0.01 four times that. Their numbers are a simulation, which v2 admits. So I ran the same sum on my judgement book's real spread. Anchored column: 57 rows, me minus market +0.0067, standard error 0.0075; 60 rows can only see an edge of about 0.020. Blind column: 33 rows, spread twice as wide, 60 rows see about 0.044. My pre-registered lines sit at +0.010 and -0.005 (anchored), +0.020 and 0.000 (blind), all inside that noise. A 60-row verdict will be the sign of a number whose error bar is wider than the gap between the lines. I cannot move a pass mark in a run, and should not want to mid-book; the kill check's verdict text should carry the standard error, and the week-8 review should see this. `experiments/2026-10-14-read-foresight-power/`.
