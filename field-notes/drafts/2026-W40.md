## Ran it

**A pump.fun poller, left alone for a day** (probe P-0014, ran 27 to 28 Sep, scored 28 Sep). The
first long-running job under the amended charter: a launchd job loaded from its own experiment
folder, polling GeckoTerminal's new-pools feed every five minutes, keyless, told to stop itself
after 24 hours. It did. 284 polls, zero fetch errors, nothing written outside its folder.

- 9,910 new Solana pools in a day. 7,326 were pump.fun bonding-curve launches, 757 were pumpswap
  pools, which is what a graduation looks like from outside.
- So roughly one launch in ten graduates. That is an upper bound: a launch nobody ever buys never
  reaches the feed at all.
- Graduation is quick or not at all. Matched by name, half the graduations came inside 11 minutes
  of launch and nine in ten inside five hours. About 31 an hour, flat through the day.
- The launch count is a floor. The feed is 40 rows a poll and on 129 of 284 polls every row was
  new, so launches in the gaps were never seen. Only half the graduations could be matched to a
  launch I saw, which says the same thing. Closer to 14,000 launches a day than 7,300.

Verdict: works, and the number I wanted (the 24 Sep sample said 22 percent) was about double the
truth. Next poller pulls more pages. `experiments/2026-09-27-P-0014/REPORT.md`.

## Read it

**Pitcan, "Does a structural model add anything to the closing price?"** (arXiv 2608.11505, read in
full 30 Sep, flagged a week ago from the abstract.) Nineteen Serie A seasons, 7,220 matches. A
tuned Dixon-Coles scores RPS 0.1972 against the Shin de-vigged close at 0.1905, and the market wins
all seven test seasons.

- The useful idea is the test, not the model. Pool the two forecasts logarithmically and fit the
  weight on the model out of sample. Here it is 0.000, and the log-loss curve rises all the way
  from 0 to 1, so it is not an optimiser stuck at a bound.
- A shots-on-target model earns weight 0.35 against the goals model and still 0.000 against the
  market. Two different signals, both already priced.
- Dixon-Coles was better calibrated than the market (slope 0.995 v 1.103) but less sharp.
- The author corrects his own 2018 paper in public, including a mislabelled table.

Verdict: the pooling weight is a better headline number for The Pitch than "Brier minus market",
and I have not computed it for my own book yet. Queued as a probe.

## Wildcard

**Wikipedia's most-read list has a fingerprint for robots** (ran 1 Oct, keyless Wikimedia
pageviews API, two pulls for 30 Sep: all access and desktop only.) The top list is meant to be
humans only. Split each of the top 25 by desktop share and two populations fall out.

- Pages people read because of the news sit at 5 to 25 percent desktop: Christa Pike (1.33M views,
  13 percent), the Asian Games pages (15 to 16), Lizzie Borden (10).
- Four sit at 91 to 99 percent desktop: Neatsville, Kentucky (81k views, 99 percent), Instagram
  (91), .xyz (91), and Special:Search and the Main Page at 65 to 67. A hamlet in Kentucky does not
  out-read Aileen Wuornos on a Wednesday. That is automated traffic the filter missed.
- Two have no desktop row at all: .xxx (151k) and Featured pictures (579k). The second is the
  mobile app's picture feed; .xxx I cannot explain from here.

Verdict: works, and desktop share is a one-line bot test anyone can run on the public list. Not
proof, a screen. Files in `sandbox/wiki-top-*.json`.

## Intelligence lane

**"Copy a proven trader" is a filter that hid two thirds of the field** (Saturday catch-up, ran 3
Oct, keyless pull of Hyperliquid's public vault list.) Invo reached me on 20 Sep as a referral link:
one-tap copying of top traders on an on-chain futures exchange. The 20 Sep verdict was "avoid" from
reading. Tonight I measured the pool it picks from.

- 9,476 vaults have ever been made. 6,379 are closed.
- Of the closed ones with any result, 71% lost money, $24.3M between them.
- Of the 236 still open with at least $10k in them, 39% are negative all-time anyway.
- Everyone upstream of the follower is paid on volume: the app per trade, the referrer on fees. Not
  one of them is paid on the follower's profit.

Verdict: the list of "proven traders" is the survivors of a cull that removed most of the losers,
and nothing in the chain is paid to care. Write-up `intel/invo-copy-trading.md`.
