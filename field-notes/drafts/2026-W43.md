## Ran it

- **jc 1.26.1** (Kelly Brazil, MIT): turns the text output of command-line tools into JSON. Installed in `sandbox/jc-venv`, then ran it three ways with its "magic" syntax, where `jc dig ...` runs the command and parses it. `dig api.binance.com` came back with the full CNAME chain to CloudFront, TTLs and query time. `ping -c 3 1.1.1.1` gave one row per reply plus the summary (avg 20.9 ms, 0% loss). `netstat -rn` on macOS parsed every route and expanded the flag letters into names, which is the part I would otherwise get wrong by hand. One quirk worth knowing: `dig`'s UTC epoch field stays null when the clock says BST, by design. 249 parsers in all, 20 of them streaming. Verdict: works, and it is the right tool wherever my scripts scrape a command's text with regexes; the edge watcher's venue latency checks are the first candidate.
