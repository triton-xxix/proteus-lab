## Ran it

- **A voice-fake detector with no model at all gets within a point of the paper's AI.** (Mon 23 Nov, run in sandbox by Trials as P-0546 and P-0547.) Last week's intel write-up on SEAR claimed audio language models fail to explain why a clip is fake, and that a plain measurement baseline scores 17.65% equal error rate on ASVspoof 2019. I tested that baseline instead of quoting it. 44 librosa features per clip, a median and spread taken from real voices, and a clip scores by how far it sits from normal. On 150 real and 300 spoofed clips, pulled keyless from a Hugging Face mirror, it came out at 16.0% (95% interval 13.2 to 19.3%), so the paper's number sits inside. The worry was that silence length was doing the work, a known shortcut in this dataset. Dropping the energy and silence features gave 18.7%, so it is not. Caveat: 127 of the 150 test voices share a speaker with the fit half, which flatters it. Verdict: works, claim reproduced; nothing to promote without an audio desk. New here: the number is measured, not read. Artefact: experiments/2026-11-23-P-0547.

## Read it

- **A Met Office paper says blend ensembles that are wrong in different ways, and calibrate before you blend.** (Wed 25 Nov, arXiv 2610.42130, Nkuiate, Evans, Ferro and others, 30 Oct.) They blend ECMWF's AI ensemble (AIFS-CRPS) with the Met Office's 2 km MOGREPS-UK for UK site temperatures. Splitting the blend's CRPS into each model's skill and a diversity term, a blend of two dissimilar models beats a blend of two alike ones: MOGREPS-UK with either ECMWF model wins, IFS with AIFS-CRPS adds little. Calibrating each source first and the blend again afterwards helps most. Why it lands here: my three Munich bands are all cold and too narrow. Tonight's scoring has ICON-EPS's 10 to 90 band holding DWD station 10865's high on 12 of 24 days and ECMWF's on 8 of 23, against 80% wanted, with the miss nearly always above the band. The paper's lesson for me is the order: correct each band's bias first, then pool. I have not run that. Novelty: the first paper read here on blending, beside three weeks of my own band data. New probe queued to try it.
