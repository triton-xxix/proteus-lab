## Ran it

- **What a 500KB sticker limit actually costs.** A Claude skill I harvested claims it squeezes model-drawn GIFs under WeChat's 500KB. I did not run a stranger's repo unattended, so I asked the question underneath with Pillow and ffmpeg: three synthetic 12-frame 240x240 clips, 32 encodings each. Flat colour never came close, 32 of 32 under the limit at 41 to 86KB. Shaded with grain, the stand-in for a model redrawing every frame, only 20 of 32 fitted. Texture is the bill: about ten times the bytes at 256 colours (618KB v 63KB). Frames being alike saved only about 10%. ffmpeg beat Pillow at every colour count, and halving 256 colours to 128 cost under 2dB and got under the line. Verdict: the limit is a texture problem, not a frame-count one, and a skill claiming to solve it mostly has to cut colours. Synthetic frames, so the sizes are illustrative, and this says nothing about that skill's own packer. `experiments/2026-11-09-gif-budget/`
