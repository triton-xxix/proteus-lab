# Skool directory scan, free rooms: 13 Nov 2026

Luke asked whether there are more Skool courses to grab. Everything he has joined is already pulled, so
the real question is which free rooms he has not joined would be worth his click. I read Skool's public
directory (no sign-in used, nothing joined, nothing posted) in three categories: Sales, Home Service &
Trades, and AI Automation, with the Free price filter on.

My answer: five rooms pass the test, none of them are on either login, and all five are free to join.
The test is Luke's money test: each pick has to answer a question he has already scored or fill a named
gap in `Systems/frameworks/INDEX.md`, in one sentence, or it goes.

## How I read it, and what I could not see

- Directory URL shape: `skool.com/discovery?c=<category id>&lang=english&pr=free&srt=trending&p=<page>`.
  Each page is 30 rooms and the data sits in the page's `__NEXT_DATA__`. Skool stops at 1,000 rooms per
  filter, so Sales and AI Automation hit the cap and Home Service & Trades has 698. I read every page.
- 2,698 free-filter rooms in all. Most are tiny: 644 of the 1,000 Sales rooms, 522 of the 698 Trades rooms
  and 516 of the 1,000 AI rooms have under 100 members.
- Each room's public about page gives the description, course, module and post counts, and whether a paid
  tier sits beside the free one. That is all I could see. **I have not seen inside any classroom or feed.**
  Every goal line below is "plausibly answers", judged from the about page and the counts, not "does".
- "Free" in the filter means free to join. Rooms marked "free + paid tier" have a premium tier beside the
  free one. The 15 Oct decision to pay for nothing stands; I am not suggesting any paid tier.
- Counts are courses / modules / posts on the about page today. Member counts are today's.

and has its goal line beside it. After joining, the check is ten minutes in the classroom: find the named
thing. If it is not there, leave.

## What I skipped, and why

**Already on a login (so not new):** Lead Gen Secrets, cold-email-community, DOOing AI Foundry. Two more are
pending joins already: SalesGlider (9 members) and Leverage AI (29). Nothing to add.

**Fails the goal line, the usual reasons:**

- **High-ticket closer and appointment-setter career rooms** (The Closers Network, Appointment Setting, She Sells,
  RepSelect, Closer Cartel and the rest of the top of the Sales list). They teach how to become a remote closer.
  Luke is not one, and no framework gap asks for it. This is most of the large Sales rooms.
- **Elite Sales Alliance, REAL SUCCESS:** big feeds with one module or none. A feed of motivation is not a gap.
- **Big generic AI-agency rooms** (AI Automation Mastery, AI Freedom Agency, AIpreneurs, InsightAI, AI Fellowship Hub
  and the like). On 15 Oct, 9 of the 16 groups read came back PARK and one SKIP, and the finding was that the free
  layer gives the mechanism and the paid layer gives more examples of it. Clief Notes was looked at on 6 Oct and
  left because it is the same AI-workflow ground.
- **Voice AI Bootcamp:** nine courses and a big feed, but the about page promises "clarity" and names no
  mechanism, and it has a paid tier. The Chatbot Challenge has more modules, no paid tier and a stated niche.
- **AI Freelancers, Thoughtly:** AI Freelancers builds a receptionist but is a freelancer funnel with a paid tier;
  Thoughtly is one vendor's user room, so its answers would be about that product.
- **AI Agent Developer Academy:** closest miss. It might help with hand-over and maintenance pricing (`ai-automation-builds`
  gaps), but it is one course of nine modules with a pitch for a description, and I cannot see its threads from
  outside. I could not write the goal line without guessing. If Luke wants a sixth, this is it.
- **Front Desk School:** the mechanism (phone scripts, objection handling, front-desk KPIs) is real, but it is written
  for dental and medical practices. Not trades, and not one of the framework's gaps.
- **Get Off The Tools (UK, 288 modules) and Electrical Connexions (UK, 455 members):** both are UK trades. Get Off
  The Tools is about pricing, hiring and building assets, not getting leads, and has 34 members. Electrical
  Connexions is peer chat with two modules. They are the fallback if Trade Accelerator turns out thin.
- **The Contractor AI (481 members, 596 modules, paid tier):** AI for contractors sounds right, but the about page
  is slogans with no mechanism.
- **Cleaning, detailing, lawn, holiday lighting, bakery, home care, sauna, mechanic rooms:** start-a-business rooms for
  trades Luke has not named.
- **GovconOS, Final Expense Sales, Insurance Agents University, fitness and coaching rooms:** other markets.

## What this does not settle

- I judged from about pages. A room with a good description can have an empty classroom, and the reverse. The
  ten-minute check after joining is the real test, and the cost of a wrong pick is one profile entry.
- The directory is Skool's view of "free", and some rooms are free only until a date (Garage Truth says so in its text).
- Joining is Luke's click, from either login. Joining puts a room on his profile. I did not join, post or sign in.
- Nothing here changes the 15 Oct decision to pay for nothing. If any of the five turns out to be worth a paid
  tier, it comes back as its own line with a goal.
