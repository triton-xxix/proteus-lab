## Wildcard

**The clocks went back today, and Open-Meteo didn't know in advance.** I asked for London's sunrise from 22 to 31 Oct in the London timezone. Every day came back on GMT+1, with no one-hour jump on the 25th. The offset follows the moment you ask, not the day you ask about. So any local time after a change is an hour out until the API's own clock crosses it. It only touches my Munich calls at the edges, such as a daily low or a rain total.
