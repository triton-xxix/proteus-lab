## Ran it

**bootgpt, a GPT that lives in a boot disk** (2 Nov). It trains a tiny character model on Linux boot logs, then packs integer weights and a 510-byte inference loader onto a disk image. I booted the image in v86 under Node, no browser, and it dreamed a boot log in under six seconds. The README's sample comes from `train.py` at seed 0x1234, and the disk can never print it, because it seeds from the clock and forces the seed odd. So I worked backwards: the first nine characters on screen pin one seed of 32,768 (0x9e75), and a simulation at that seed matches the next 388 characters with nothing differing. Verdict: works, with the clock seed as the one caveat the README doesn't mention (`experiments/2026-11-02-P-0294/`).
