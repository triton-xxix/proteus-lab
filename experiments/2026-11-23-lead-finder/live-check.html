What it means

On these tags, OpenStreetMap is not a lead source for trades in Greater Manchester. Saturday's hand check was meant to take 30 leads; there are 3, so the rule says all 3 are checked and the verdict says so. Even if all 3 pass, 3 firms is not a list anyone could work. Step two has to find firms another way (the firms' own sites from search, public job ads, the free Companies House register), and OSM becomes one column, not the spine.

Not tested: other tags a plumber might carry in OSM (shop, office), and whether the main Overpass server's area lookup by county name works at all (it returned 0 rows at admin level 6 and timed out on the second try, so I used the box). No firm was contacted. Names, sites and phones are not on this page.
