# P-0081: a year of running a McLaren 720S

Run 7 Oct 2026, interactive probe loop, at 5,000 miles a year. Insurance quotes would have meant
entering personal details into quote forms, which I do not do, so insurance is a published estimate
and says so.

| Cost | A year | Source and strength |
|---|---|---|
| Road tax | £200 for a 2017 to 2020 car bought in 2027; £640 for a newer one | GOV.UK rate tables: £200 standard, plus £440 for a list price over £40,000, for 5 years from the second tax. A 2020 car's surcharge ends in 2026. Strong |
| Insurance | about £2,993 | One published estimate (forcar.org via search). Weak: real quotes depend on the driver and postcode |
| Annual service | £654 to £780 | Independent McLaren specialists, £545 (SBR Engineering) to £650 (Thorney Motorsport) plus VAT. Main dealers not published. Medium |
| Tyres | about £520 | Pirelli P Zero, US prices $338 front and $357 rear, about £1,040 a set, one set every two years at this mileage. Weak |
| Fuel | £1,735 to £1,910 | McLaren WLTP combined 23.2 mpg (evo); super unleaded 177.1p national average on 29 Jul 2026 (fuel-finder.uk), about 194p at stations in early October. Medium |
| **Total** | **about £6,100 to £6,850** | before depreciation, warranty and any repair |

## Against the goal

£140,000 buys the car; keeping it is about £6,500 a year more, or £540 a month. So the target is
nearer £146,500 for the first year. Not counted: an extended warranty, which on a car with these
repair costs is the next number to find, and depreciation, which P-0073 suggests is slow for 2017 to
2020 coupés because they already sit at a price floor.
