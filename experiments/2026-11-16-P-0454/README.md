  span (since 1993) but no count.
- That one says just over half within five sessions. P-0433 measured 84% of SPY opening gaps
  filled within five days since 2009. The gap is probably definition: the Short's chart is about
  fair value gaps (a three-candle imbalance), which are not opening gaps, and the search mixes the
  two (3 of 6 results are fair value gap videos).
- The two long videos, the ones with money in the title, give no number at all.

Verdict: works. Of the gap videos a search serves, most state nothing checkable; the one that
does is more modest than the 85% Short, and measures a different kind of gap.
