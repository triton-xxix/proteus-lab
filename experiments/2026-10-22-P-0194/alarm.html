Playlist Alarm

Wakes you with your own .m3u or .m3u8 playlist. One file, no install, nothing leaves your machine.

Browsers cannot follow paths inside a playlist on their own, so pick the songs too (select all in the folder). Entries that are web addresses play without this.
Load a playlist to begin.
    Keep this tab open and the computer awake. Setting the alarm with a click is what lets the browser play sound later.
