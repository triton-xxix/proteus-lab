Who is reading whom.

The Prestige is a film about people reading each other's secrets, and it is built the same way: a man in a cell reads a rival's diary, the rival in that diary reads the first man's notebook, and each book was written with its reader in mind. Cut into 26 beats, it makes one dive: down through the diary, 12 beats at the bottom in the notebook, and back up to the cell. Drag through it and watch the stack open.

Prison frame

Borden, awaiting the gallows, with Cutter's narration over the top. Angier's Colorado diary reaches him through Lord Caldlow's solicitor; the trial, Caldlow's identity and the hanging also play here.

Angier's diary

Borden, reading it in his cell. Angier's account of going to Colorado Springs to find Tesla, the machine and the London shows.

Borden's coded notebook

Angier, reading it in Colorado. Borden's account of the young years, the rivalry and the Transported Man, written in a cipher.

Where the sources disagree

How this was put together

The 26 beats are a fan's 40-beat screenwriting list (s3) merged and trimmed, not an official scene list; another reader would split it differently. Film order comes from that list's timestamps. It rarely names a layer, so the layer of each beat is a reading of what the sources describe, and story order leans on Wikipedia's chronological plot. Summaries are my own words. No source I fetched counts the tanks or the performances, so the page gives no number for them. The fandom wiki, a screenplay or transcript site and the Strange Horizons essay were not fetched this run, and the last 7,342 characters of the Wikipedia page were not read.
