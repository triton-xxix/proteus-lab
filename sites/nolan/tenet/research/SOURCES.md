# Sources for the Tenet timeline

Graded A (the film's own text, or an encyclopaedic article with citations), B (a reputable explainer,
used for ordering, mechanics and the few durations the dialogue gives), C (fan material, cross-check
only). Accessed 7 October 2026 by Proteus, by fetching the page. Nothing below is written from memory
of the film; where memory and a source disagreed, the source won, and where no source could be found
the page says "unstated".

The published screenplay (Faber, 2020) was not read: there is no copy on this machine, and none of
the sources below cite it by page. It is therefore not a source here.

## A

- A1. "Tenet (film)", Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenet_(film). The Plot section (31
  events in watch order), the Sator-square paragraph (its ref 183), the red and blue teams and the
  ten minutes (ref 184), the filming locations by scene, release, runtime, budget, gross, crew, cast,
  and the on-screen date "the 14th" for both the Kyiv opera siege and the Stalsk-12 detonation.
- A2. "Christopher Nolan filmography", Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan_filmography.
  The thirteen features and their years, including The Odyssey (2026).

## B

- B1. "The Most Confusing Moments In Tenet, Explained", /Film.
  https://www.slashfilm.com/1563140/most-confusing-moments-in-tenet-explained/. Confirms the 14th for
  both the opera and Stalsk-12; the turnstile as the inversion device; hypothermia rather than burns
  for an inverted explosion; Team Blue inverted and wearing breathing apparatus; the Sator square.
- B2. "The Entire Tenet Timeline Explained", Looper.
  https://www.looper.com/769196/the-entire-tenet-timeline-explained/. The only source found that
  states the two durations the dialogue gives: "days inverted" for Kat's wound to heal, and the
  Freeport turnstile being "a week earlier". Also each character's inversion points, and it flags
  its own speculation.
- B3. "The 'Tenet' Movie Explained", No Film School. https://nofilmschool.com/tenet-explained.
  The red team forward and the blue team inverted, the blue team starting ten minutes in the future;
  the inverted explosion becoming cold; the future's motive (climate); the grandfather paradox;
  Sator's cancer. Flags the "Neil is Max" theory as a theory.
- B4. "Tenet: Robert Pattinson's Neil Timeline Explained", Den of Geek.
  https://www.denofgeek.com/movies/tenet-robert-pattinson-neil-timeline/. Neil's recruitment by the
  Protagonist "years from now" (dialogue), Neil already at the opera, several Neils at Stalsk-12,
  Neil dying inverted at the gate. Its recruitment timing is marked as the author's inference.
- B5. "Tenet ending explained", GamesRadar. https://www.gamesradar.com/tenet-ending-explained/.
  The fetch was truncated; only a search snippet was usable, carrying Neil's line "What's happened's
  happened" with his gloss that it is "an expression of faith in the mechanics of the world".
