## Days

The film names one day: the 14th, for the opera, Sator's Vietnam suicide and the Stalsk-12
detonation (A1). Dialogue gives two durations: Kat needs days inverted (B2), and the Oslo turnstile
is about a week before Tallinn (B2). Everything else is unstated. The page therefore carries a
world **order** for every event and a world **day** only where one of those three facts fixes it.
No day numbers are invented.

## Where this model is a reading, not a fact

- The Protagonist's third presence at Oslo (re-inverted, chased by Neil) is inferred from plot 9
  and 17; the film shows it rather than saying it.
- Which Sator is inverted on the Tallinn highway, and when he inverted, is contested in the
  explainers; the page says "an inverted Sator" and no more.
- Where the Protagonist re-inverts before the pincer (he is red, so he must), is unstated.
- Whether the operative at the opera is inverted or forward at that moment is contested; the page
  says only that it is Neil (inferred) and that an inverted bullet is seen.
