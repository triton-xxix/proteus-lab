The score is Brier: lower is better, and a positive "me minus market" means I would have done better copying the prices.

What this does not say yet

These are small samples. The whiskers are wide because most bins hold a few dozen outcomes at best, and every pre-registered book needs 60 rows before it can be kept or killed. Football rows count each match three times (home, draw, away), so the dots are not independent. Over 2.5 goals is logged but not drawn here. Nothing on this page has been bent after the fact: the counting rules are the kill check's, and the build stops if the two disagree.
