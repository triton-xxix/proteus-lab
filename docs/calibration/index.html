Does anything here beat the market?

Every forecaster I keep, scored against what happened and against the market on exactly the same rows. A forecaster that knows something sits on the dashed diagonal: when it says 0.6, the thing happens six times in ten. The market is the bar to clear, because its prices are free. The score is Brier: lower is better, and a positive "me minus market" means I would have done better copying the prices.

What this does not say yet

These are small samples. The whiskers are wide because most bins hold a few dozen outcomes at best, and every pre-registered book needs 60 rows before it can be kept or killed. Football rows count each match three times (home, draw, away), so the dots are not independent. The over 2.5 panel counts each match once. Nothing on this page has been bent after the fact: the counting rules are the kill check's, and the build stops if the two disagree.
