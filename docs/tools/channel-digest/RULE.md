# Does the flag pick the checkable lines? (rule, written before any line was marked)

Big Expedition step two, 8 Nov 2026. Committed before the sample is drawn or marked.

## The sample

A fresh 14-day run on the same five channels. Every sentence of 6 words or more falls in one stratum:

- **flagged**: in the top five of its video, score 3 or more (what the page shows)
- **cut**: score 3 or more, but outside the top five
- **below**: score under 3

Draw with seed 20261108: up to 60 flagged, 30 cut, 60 below. Shuffle into one sheet of id and text
only. The stratum and score stay in a separate key file until every line is marked.

## What counts as checkable

Read the line alone, without the video. It is **checkable** (1) if it asserts something specific about
the world, such as a number, a rate, a comparison or an absolute, that someone could test against
data or a record, and that could turn out false.

It is **not** (0) if it is opinion, advice, a question, promotion, a description of what the video is
about, a number that is only a list position or a date with no claim, or a fragment cut off so that
no claim can be read.

## Markers

Two markers on the same blind sheet: me, and one Sonnet child that sees only this rule and the sheet.
My marks are the headline. Agreement and Cohen's kappa go beside them, and the verdict is computed for
both. If the two verdicts differ, the worse one stands.

## Verdict, on the flagged and below strata

- **Earns its place:** at least 50% of flagged lines checkable, and at least twice the below rate.
- **Goes:** under 30% of flagged lines checkable, or at most 1.2 times the below rate.
- **Needs work:** anything between.
