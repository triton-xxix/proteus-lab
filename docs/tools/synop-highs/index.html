Yesterday's high at any airport, without a key

Free observation feeds go stale: GHCN daily stops in August 2025 and Meteostat's bulk files stopped in March 2026 for Heathrow. Ogimet keeps every SYNOP report up to the hour, and the 18 UTC report carries the day's maximum. This one file turns that into a date,tmax CSV.

Run on 18 UTC reports on 4 Nov 2026: Heathrow (03772) and Strasbourg-Entzheim (07190), 64 days each, 2026-09-01 to 2026-11-03, no day missing.

HeathrowStrasbourg

Does it match the official record?

At Strasbourg I have Météo-France's own daily TX for the same station (the file P-0324 used). Of 63 days, the SYNOP high is the same to the tenth on 63, within half a degree on 63, and lower on 0: mean +0.00°C. Every day the same. I expected a few low days, since the group covers 06 to 18 UTC and a warm evening would sit outside it; in this autumn none did. The UK's group has the same window, unchecked against the Met Office's own record.

SYNOP high minus TX per day. Orange: identical; dark: different. Hover a dot for the two values.

Use it

python3 synop_highs.py --wmo 03772 --start 2026-09 --end 2026-11 --csv heathrow.csv
python3 bias_check.py --lat 51.47 --lon -0.4543 --csv heathrow.csv --split 2026-10-15

One request per month, two seconds apart; standard library only. --hour picks another report if your country puts its maximum elsewhere: the tool prints how many reports at that hour lacked a max group, so a wrong hour shows at once. At Heathrow 249 of the 18 UTC lines had none (duplicates and corrections in the feed; every day still had one). It feeds bias-check's --csv straight in.
