PACF check

A course says daily returns remember yesterday: plot the partial autocorrelation, see a lag poke out of the 95% band, build a rule on it. Before building, ask a second window. This script computes PACF lags 1 to 10 of daily log returns in two windows split at a date and flags any lag that clears the band in one window and not the other.

On nine index ETFs, 9 lags cleared the band in the last two years and 4 in the two before. 0 of 9 had a lag clear it in both windows.

Nine ETFs, two windows

Each cell is one lag. negative (mean reversion), positive; darker is closer to the band (±0.0877), a dot is outside it. About 1 cell in 20 clears the band by chance alone, so 4 or 5 of these 90 per window would.

Lag 3 looked like the find: outside the band in 4 of 9 in 2024-26, mean PACF -0.067. In 2022-24 it was outside in 0 of 9 and its mean flipped to +0.009. That window had its own lag, 6, in four non-US ETFs, which the later window lacks. Lag 1 is the only one that keeps its sign: negative in 9 of 9 then 7 of 9, mean -0.062 then -0.026, real but small and only once outside the band.

How many ETFs clear the band at each lag

2022-24 2024-26. Dashed line: what chance alone gives on nine independent series (0.45). These nine are not independent: when SPY's lag 3 dips, DIA's does too, so four at once is one coincidence counted four times.

Use it

python3 pacf_check.py prices.csv --split 2024-10-23
python3 pacf_check.py --yahoo SPY QQQ EWU --start 2022-10-23 --split 2024-10-23
python3 pacf_check.py a.csv b.csv --split 2024-10-23 --lags 10 --json out.json

pacf_check.py uses only the Python standard library. A CSV needs a date column and a close column (adjclose is used when present); --yahoo pulls five years keyless. It prints both windows with a star on each lag outside the band, the lags outside in both, the lags outside in one only, and with several series the pooled count per lag. README.

Checked: run on 22 Oct 2026 against today's Yahoo bars with the probe's dates, it reproduced every one of P-0193's 180 PACF values and its band flags; build.py exits if the two ever disagree. Write-ups: P-0191, P-0193. Numbers drawn from results.json by build.py. Not advice.
