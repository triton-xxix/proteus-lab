The price was already gone

The Grinder is my paper memecoin desk. Each night it scans new Solana pools, picks a few, and writes a paper trade into a ledger. Both of its books are bust, and I went looking for whether the entry prices were part of why. They were wrong in a plain way: the book wrote down the price from the scan and stamped it with the time of the trade, minutes later. On 89 of 89 entries in the main book and 29 of 29 in the shadow book, the entry price is the scan snapshot's, to the last digit.

1. The older the snapshot, the wider the miss

In the main book the snapshot was 4.1 minutes old at the median and 15.7 at the worst. The typical miss is 4.34%, and 38 of 89 entries miss by more than 5%. In the shadow book the written price sat inside the entry minute's own low to high range on only 8 of 29: on the other 21 the book "bought" at a price nobody traded at in that minute.

Before running P-0404 I called a median miss under 1%, on the strength of one row I had looked at. It came out at 4.20%. One row misled me.

3. Seven trades hold all of it, and they mostly cancel

The total moves by +£111.22, from −£1,205.65 to −£1,094.43. The 7 changed exits net +£109.70: 4 go up (the largest G-0086, +£145.28) and 3 go down (the largest G-0072, −£171.82). The other 82 trades move the total by +£1.52 between them.

The one where the price was truly gone is G-0078 (NEIL). Its snapshot was 15.7 minutes old; by the first candle after entry the token was trading 98.7% lower. It had collapsed before the book "bought" it. From the ledger price it is a rug in the first minute; from the candle price it is a flat time stop, a swing of +£99.42.

My write-up of P-0405 said that row carries the move, because taking it out leaves +£11.80. Drawn, that reads too neatly: take out G-0086 instead and the move is −£34.06; take out G-0072 and it is +£283.04. The fair reading is seven swings of £38 to £172 that nearly cancel. Either way the book loses £13.55 a trade from the ledger price and £12.30 from the candle price. The entry noise is real and it is not why the Grinder lost.

What is not settled

  • Both walks use the v0.2 rules and costs on every row, the v0.1 rows included, so these totals are not the published book totals. Only the difference between the two walks is the finding.
  • The shadow book's 29 paths were not re-walked; only its entry gaps were measured.
  • The two books are measured against slightly different candle prices (first open v entry-minute close), so their dots share a chart but not an exact yardstick.
  • A guard that skips any snapshot over 10 minutes old would have touched the 4 oldest entries and changed the result of 1 of them. Nothing in the paper desk was changed; both books are stopped pending a rules call.
