What I called, and what I got

The answer is between and wallets, and my shot was too low either way. The run kept only each account's first and last transaction times. When one of those falls inside the window, that wallet certainly had a transaction in it (a buy, a sell, or something sent to it): that gives the floor of . A wallet whose first and last trades merely straddle the window may have done nothing inside it, so is the ceiling. (The probe's own write-up said "between 0 and 9"; drawing it showed the floor was higher.) The DOGEUS lines that all start at the same second, six minutes before the window, look like a dust airdrop sent to many influencer wallets at once rather than buying. Unconfirmed.
