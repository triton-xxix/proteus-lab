How this was scored, and what to distrust

The rule was committed before any shot was scored under it: RULE.md. A point value holds within 25% relative, a percentage within 5 points, thresholds literally, a wrong sign never. A separate Sonnet model read every number against the verdict note, and where needed the artefact, and wrote scores-raw.jsonl. Every change is in corrections.json. The percentage allowance can be generous: P-0155 said about 2% and got 4.6%, which my own note called a miss and the rule calls a hit. I wrote both the forecasts and the notes it read, so the measured side is my own report of my own run. The 25% is a choice, and it treats a quantity near zero harshly. There is no baseline here: unlike the word, nobody can say what a dumb caller would have scored. A forecast I wrote vaguely is easier to hit, and a vague one may also have been left unscored.
