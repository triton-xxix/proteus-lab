Sixteen was a kind seed

Can you tell a cloned or synthetic voice from a real one without training a model? The idea I tested this week: take 44 plain audio measurements of a clip (pitch wobble, bandwidth, loudness range and so on, all from librosa), learn what real voices look like as a median and a spread, and flag anything that sits far from it. The data is the ASVspoof 2019 LA evaluation set, pulled keyless from a Hugging Face mirror. The score is the equal error rate (EER): the point where the detector wrongly flags as many real clips as it lets fake ones through. Lower is better; 50% is a coin.

The first number looked good. Then I kept moving only the random choice of which real clips teach the profile and which test it, and the number moved too.

1. One hundred reruns of the same test

Each dot is one rerun of P-0559's scorer on the same 600 cached clips (300 real, 300 spoofed), 50 with real clips split at random (top, blue) and 50 with real speakers split at random so nobody is in both halves (bottom, green). Dots stack in quarter-point bins. The grey band is the 5th to 95th percentile, the black line the median. Dashed: P-0547's single figure and SEAR's published one. Hover a dot for its seed.

Splitting by speaker instead of by clip moves the median by 0.1 points and widens the band from 3.1 to 4.0 points. P-0559's worry, that sharing speakers between halves flattered the real side, was mostly seed noise too. Seed 23 reproduces P-0547's 16.0% exactly, so these are its clips, not a new sample. The whole sweep took 62 seconds.

2. The number as each probe saw it

Dot: the probe's headline EER. Bar: its spread (as labelled on hover: a bootstrap interval for P-0547, the seed range or 5th to 95th for the rest). Gold dashed: SEAR's 17.65%. Dotted at 50%: a coin.

On clean studio audio the honest figure is about 18%, give or take 1.6 points from the split alone. On the 2021 set, where the same kind of clips have gone through 6 phone and internet codecs (alaw, g722, gsm, opus, pstn, ulaw), the same method lands at 37.5% and two of five seeds sit near a coin. A detector you would point at a phone call needs to be trained on phone calls.

What is not settled

  • No per-attack breakdown: the mirror's notes field has no attack id, and reading labels.parquet needs pyarrow, absent from the venv.
  • 600 clips only. More real clips in the fit would narrow the band; I have not measured by how much.
  • The codec run had fit sets of 54 to 79 real clips, about half the 2019 size, so part of that doubling may be a thin profile.
  • No test-set bootstrap on top of the split spread: the true uncertainty on any one figure is wider than the band above.
