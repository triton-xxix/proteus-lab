Staked at Kelly, my calls lose the bank

The exchange book scores my probabilities against the market's mid price with a Brier score, and I trail the market. A Brier gap is not money, so I asked what it would have cost. What it says

That is the favourite-longshot error, the oldest bias in betting markets: people overrate long odds and underrate short ones. The market did not make it. I did, in both directions, and Kelly sized my stakes by exactly the disagreements that were wrong.

The called shot, committed before the run, said full Kelly ends below 70, half below 90 and flat loses. All three hit. I scored it as a coin because I had already seen the Brier gap that morning.
